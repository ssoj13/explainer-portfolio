## Overview

An interactive Apple-style React Director's Console & Multi-Model AI Production Portfolio created for the 30-second commercial and technical documentary ***The 21 Empty Rows*** (investigating a 6-year `iCloudPhotos.exe` 100% single-core CPU infinite loop caused by 21 `NULL` rows and `SQLite[2067]` constraint collisions).

### Key Features
- **3-Engine Neural Shootout**: Compare **Google Nano Banana Pro** (`google/nano-banana-pro`), **OpenAI GPT-Image-2** (`openai/gpt-image-2` High Quality), and **Runway Gen-4 Image** (`runway/gen-4` 1920x1088) side-by-side or individually.
- **20-Persona Casting Portfolio (`#01` – `#20`)**: 60 high-resolution cinema portraits across 5 character archetypes (Lead Systems Architects, Inside-the-Machine Clerks & Bug Author, Surgical AI Agents, Cupertino Executives, and Real-World Marina del Rey & SCE Grid).
- **3 Distinct Residential Environments (`Shots 01–06`)**:
  - **ENV A (`04_apple_style_casting_and_production/`)**: Warm California Oceanfront Penthouse in Marina del Rey (`Nano Banana Pro`).
  - **ENV B (`05_gpt_image_2_apple_style/`)**: Iconic Mid-Century Modern Hollywood Hills Glass Villa (`OpenAI GPT-Image-2`).
  - **ENV C (`06_gen_4_image_apple_style/`)**: Sunlit Two-Story Venice Beach Brick & Timber Artist Loft (`Runway Gen-4`).
- **Full Reproducible Provenance**: Every persona card and storyboard keyframe includes an organic collapsible metadata drawer displaying the exact model identifier, deterministic `seed`, `job_id`/`task_id`, resolution, SHA-256 digest, reference image chain, and exact generation prompt.
- **Interactive Director's Selection & JSON Export**: Click to choose the Lead Hero, Secondary Cast roles, per-shot keyframes, and video pipeline settings, then export `production_selection.json` with one click.
