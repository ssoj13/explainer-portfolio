# Video Production Package v1 (30 Seconds) — "The $1,000 Blank Line"

- **Duration**: `30 seconds` (6 shots × 5 seconds each — native clip length for **Runway Gen-4 / Gen-3 Alpha Turbo** and **Krea.ai Video**)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (`1920x1080` for YouTube/LinkedIn/Desktop) or `9:16` (`1080x1920` for Reels/Shorts)
- **Visual Style (Lock this as Style Reference in Krea / Runway Frames)**:
  > *Stylized cinematic 3D animation, Pixar meets David Fincher tech-noir, tactile miniature diorama inside a high-end workstation, warm tungsten and neon cyan lighting, shallow depth of field, 35mm anamorphic lens, ultra-detailed textures, 8k render.*

---

## Global Consistency Bible (Use in Krea "Style/Character Reference" or Runway "Frames")

1. **The Engine (The Workstation)**: A gleaming 12-cylinder futuristic V12 cyber-engine sitting on a desk in Marina del Rey with palm trees outside the window. Ten cylinders glow cool cyan; two cylinders are glowing scorching red-hot with steam pouring out.
2. **The Courier (The Stuck App)**: A exhausted miniature steampunk/silicon clerk wearing a sleek silver jumpsuit, sweating in a tiny mailroom, flipping through a colossal 117,000-page glowing ledger book.
3. **The AI Agent (The Fixer)**: A sleek, translucent holographic geometric drone/conductor made of golden-cyan light rays.

## Shot-by-Shot Storyboard (6 × 5s = 30s)

### Shot 01 (`00:00 – 00:05`) — The 12-Cylinder Engine at Redline
- **Visual Action**: A futuristic 12-cylinder workstation engine on a desk in Marina del Rey at sunset. Ten cylinders purr smoothly in cool blue light, while two cylinders are glowing molten red, shaking violently and venting steam.
- **Voiceover (EN)**: *"My twelve-core workstation runs non-stop. Yet for six years, two cores were revving at redline day and night—doing nothing."*
- **Voiceover (RU)**: *«Мой двенадцатиядерный компьютер пашет круглосуточно. Но шесть лет подряд два его цилиндра ревели вхолостую на максимальных оборотах день и ночь.»*
- **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Миний арван хоёр цөмт компьютер тасралтгүй ажилладаг. Гэтэл зургаан жилийн турш хоёр цилиндр нь өдөр шөнөгүй хий дэмий улайстал эргэж байлаа.»*
- **Keyframe Image Prompt (Krea / Nano Banana)**:
  `Cinematic 3D animation, Pixar meets Fincher style. A futuristic 12-cylinder V12 computer engine sitting on a dark studio desk near a window overlooking Marina del Rey palm trees at sunset. Ten cylinders glow cool cyan, while two cylinders in the front are glowing molten red-orange at 100% overload, vibrating with sparks and steam. Macro 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, dramatic lighting.`
- **Runway / Krea Motion Prompt (5s)**:
  `Slow dolly zoom in toward the two glowing red-hot cylinders. The two red cylinders vibrate intensely and vent puffs of white steam while the other ten blue cylinders run smoothly. Warm sunset rays shift across the metal.`

Right behind the desk, a tall glass airlock door is jammed with thousands of glowing colorful holographic family photographs pressed against the glass trying to get through. - **Keyframe Image Prompt (Krea / Nano Banana)**:
  `Cinematic 3D animation. Inside the miniature computer room, a sleek glowing cyan-and-gold holographic AI drone hovers over the giant ledger book, projecting a laser beam that attaches glowing cyan index tabs along the book's edge.
