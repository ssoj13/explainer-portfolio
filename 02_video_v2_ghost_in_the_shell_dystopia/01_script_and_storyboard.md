# Video Production Package v2 (30 Seconds) — "GHOST IN THE CORE: The 6-Year Loop"

- **Duration**: `30 seconds` (6 shots × 5 seconds each — native clip length for **Runway Gen-4** and **Krea.ai Video**)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (`1920x1080`)
- **Visual Style (Lock this as Style Reference in Krea / Runway Frames)**:
  > *Gritty photorealistic cyberpunk dystopia, Mamoru Oshii's Ghost in the Shell (1995) meets Blade Runner 2049 (Denis Villeneuve / Roger Deakins). Brutalist wet concrete, exposed fiber-optic neural cables, CRT amber/crimson hazard HUDs, volumetric rain and industrial steam, dark teal and sodium-vapor orange color grading, 35mm Panavision anamorphic lens, film grain, dead-serious atmospheric sci-fi.*

---

## Global Consistency Bible (For Krea "Style Reference" / Runway "Frames")

1. **The Monolith Rig (The 12-Core Workstation)**: A dark, industrial liquid-cooled 12-blade cybernetic monolith standing in a rain-lashed brutalist studio overlooking coastal Los Angeles (Marina del Rey) at night. Ten blades glow cold emerald-teal; two blades are overheating in violent crimson-amber, venting high-pressure coolant steam.
2. **The Cyber-Archivist (The Stuck Process)**: A pale, cybernetically augmented synth-operator with heavy neural cables plugged into the base of his skull, trapped inside a dark claustrophobic memory vault, endlessly scanning a towering vertical holographic hex-ledger that resets every 4.00 seconds.
3. **The Autonomous Net-Agent (The Fixer)**: A sharp, non-humanoid surgical net-construct—geometric refractive black-glass blades and clinical emerald laser matrices slicing through corrupted data strata.

## Shot-by-Shot Storyboard (6 × 5s = 30s)

### Shot 01 (`00:00 – 00:05`) — Two Blades Burning in the Dark
- **Visual Action**: A dark brutalist room in Marina del Rey at night, rain streaking down tall glass windows with silhouettes of palm trees in neon smog. In the foreground, a massive 12-blade liquid-cooled cybernetic compute monolith hums. Ten blades glow calm emerald-teal; two blades sear in blinding crimson-amber, boiling their coolant tubes and hissing steam.
- **Voiceover (EN)**: *"Twelve cores running round the clock. Yet for six years, two of them were locked at maximum burn in the dark—consuming power for nothing."*
- **Voiceover (RU)**: *«Двенадцать ядер работают без остановки. Но шесть лет подряд два из них горели на пределе в темноте — сжигая энергию в пустоту.»*
- **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Арван хоёр цөм тасралтгүй ажиллана. Гэвч зургаан жилийн турш хоёр нь харанхуйд дээд хязгаартаа шатаж, эрчим хүчийг хоосон залгиж байв.»*
- **Keyframe Image Prompt**:
  `Photorealistic dark cyberpunk cinema, Ghost in the Shell 1995 and Blade Runner 2049 aesthetic, NO cartoon. A massive 12-blade liquid-cooled cybernetic server monolith inside a dark brutalist concrete studio at night. Rain on tall glass windows overlooking dark palm trees and industrial coastal lights. Ten server blades glow cold emerald-teal; two front blades glow violent molten crimson-amber at 100% thermal overload, venting thick steam around thick black neural cables. 35mm anamorphic lens, moody chiaroscuro lighting, film grain.`
- **Runway / Krea Motion Prompt (5s)**:
  `Slow ominous dolly in toward the two overheating crimson server blades. High-pressure steam hisses from the coolant pipes, crimson warning LEDs flicker off wet black cables, rain streaks down the dark window in the background.`

### Shot 02 (`00:05 – 00:10`) — The Hardwired Archivist in the 4-Second Loop
- **Visual Action**: Inside the claustrophobic subterranean data core: a pale cybernetic archivist with thick fiber-optic cables jacked into his neck sits hunched before a towering wall of scrolling amber hex-code (`117,543 RECORDS`). Every 4 seconds, a brutal crimson strobe flashes `RESET // 00:04.00`, jolting his neural harness back to zero.
- **Voiceover (EN)**: *"Deep inside the system: one daemon was lost in an endless labyrinth. Another was trapped in a four-second loop—scanning a hundred and seventeen thousand records from scratch."*
- **Voiceover (RU)**: *«Внутри системы: один демон заблудился в лабиринте директорий. Второй застрял в четырёхсекундной петле — бесконечно сканируя сто семнадцать тысяч записей с нуля.»*
- **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Системийн гүнд нэг процесс лабиринтад төөрч, нөгөө нь дөрвөн секундийн гогцоонд гацаад зуун арван долоон мянган бичлэгийг эхнээс нь тасралтгүй уншиж байлаа.»*
- **Keyframe Image Prompt**:
  `Gritty photorealistic cyberpunk, Ghost in the Shell 1995 live-action cinematic style, dark dystopia. Inside a cramped, wet brutalist data chamber, a pale cybernetic operator seen from a three-quarter rear angle with heavy black neural cables plugged into the back of his neck, hunched in front of a colossal vertical holographic terminal displaying dense amber and crimson hexadecimal database tables. A stark industrial timer on the concrete wall reads "00:04.00 LOOP". Volumetric fog, harsh CRT glow, Roger Deakins cinematography.`
- **Runway / Krea Motion Prompt (5s)**:
  `Handheld anamorphic push-in toward the cybernetic operator. The towering holographic hex columns scroll at blinding speed, then violently glitch and flash crimson as the wall timer resets to 00:04.00, sending a jolt through the neck cables.`

- **Voiceover (RU)**: *«Двадцать одна битая нулевая запись заблокировала шлюз как отравленная капсула — заперев за собой больше пяти тысяч воспоминаний на шесть лет.»*
- **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Хорин нэгэн эвдэрсэн хоосон бичлэг хаалгыг хор мэт таглаж, ард нь таван мянга гаруй дурсамжийг зургаан жилийн турш хорьжээ.»*
- **Keyframe Image Prompt**:
  `Photorealistic dark sci-fi cinema, Blade Runner 2049 and Ghost in the Shell aesthetic. Foreground extreme close-up of a transparent tactical glass terminal displaying 21 corrupted blood-red database lines reading "ASSET_ID: NULL // 0x24900000001". In the background behind a heavy industrial glass containment barrier, thousands of ethereal ghostly holographic memory fragments and photographs swirl in a dense bottleneck, pressing against the dark glass. - **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Зөвхөн нэг ширээн дээр мянган долларын Калифорнийн цахилгаан салхинд хийсжээ. Үүнийг дэлхий даяарх зуу зуун сая компьютерээр үржүүлбэл, жижигхэн алдаа дэлхийн хэмжээний зуух болон хувирна.»*

- **Voiceover (RU)**: *«За пятнадцать минут автономный ИИ-агент вскрыл бинарный код, вырезал битые узлы и выстроил новый индекс. Шестилетняя блокировка рухнула за миллисекунды.»*
- **Voiceover (MN)**: *«Арван таван минутын дотор хиймэл оюуны агент кодыг задалж, эвдэрсэн зангилаануудыг огтлоод шинэ индекс босголоо. Зургаан жилийн түгжээ миллисекундын дотор нуран унав.»*
- **Keyframe Image Prompt**:
  `Photorealistic dark sci-fi, Ghost in the Shell 1995 net-dive sequence meets Blade Runner 2049. Inside the dark brutalist server chamber, a sleek obsidian-and-emerald geometric AI construct projects razor-thin green laser grids that vaporize red corrupted data blocks and assemble a towering crystalline emerald index tree.
